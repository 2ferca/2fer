What's in a name?

It's a Canadian thing.

Since time immemorial, the unofficial Canadian currency for moving a couch, picking up a fridge, or borrowing your buddy’s truck has been a two-four.

No contracts. No invoices. Just 24 cold ones and a heartfelt “Thanks, bud.”

But times have changed. Gas costs money, trucks aren’t cheap, and apparently the bank won’t accept a case of Molson as a mortgage payment.

So we’ve modernized the arrangement: same Canadian tradition, same buddy-with-a-truck attitude, just payable in actual Canadian dollars.

“Think of it as a two-four, adjusted for inflation.”
